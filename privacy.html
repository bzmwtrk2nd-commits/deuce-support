Privacy at a glance

Deuce is designed to keep your tennis scoring information on your devices. The app does not require an account and does not sell personal information.

Information stored by Deuce

Deuce may store match information that you enter or generate, including player names, scores, match duration, dates and match history. This information is used to provide the app’s scoring and history features.

Match information is stored locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch and may be transferred between your paired devices to support Watch-to-iPhone syncing.

Information we collect

Deuce does not require account registration and is not designed to collect personal information on an external server. The app does not sell data or use personal data for advertising.

Apple may collect diagnostic, purchase or usage information under Apple’s own privacy terms when you download or use apps through the App Store.

Sharing match results

When you choose to share a match result, iOS presents the system share sheet. Deuce only shares the content you deliberately select. The receiving service is governed by its own privacy policy.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual matches in Game History, clear all Game History from the menu, or remove the app from your devices. Removing the app may delete locally stored Deuce data.

Children’s privacy

Deuce is a general-purpose sports utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an external service.
