Privacy Policy

Deuce
Deuce is designed to keep your tennis information on your devices. It does not require an account, use advertising or analytics services, or sell personal information.

Information stored by Deuce

Depending on the app version and features you use, Deuce may store information you enter or generate, including:

  • Your local profile, player names and court colours.
  • Match rules, scores, duration, dates, statistics and match history.
  • Recently used Shared Match players.
  • App preferences and connection status information.

This information is stored locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch. It is not uploaded to a Deuce account or developer-operated server.

iPhone and Apple Watch syncing

Match information may be transferred between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch so that a match can be displayed, scored and kept in sync across your devices. This transfer uses Apple-provided device communication services.

Shared Match

Shared Match connects nearby iPhones directly over a local or peer-to-peer network. To provide this feature, the devices exchange the selected player names, court colours, match settings, live scores and connection messages.

This information is exchanged between the participating devices for the match. Deuce does not send it to a developer-operated server, and the developer cannot view your Shared Match.

Permissions

Deuce may ask for the following permissions when required by a feature:

  • Local Network: to discover and connect nearby iPhones for Shared Match.
  • Camera: to scan a Shared Match QR code. Deuce does not save photographs or record video.
  • Notifications: to provide relevant match or Apple Watch prompts where supported. You can control notifications in system Settings.

Optional support purchases

Deuce may offer optional consumable purchases that support its continued development. Apple processes these purchases through the App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment-card or bank details. Purchases do not unlock match features.

Diagnostic and support reports

If you choose Send Diagnostic Report, Deuce prepares an email in your default mail app. It is not sent automatically. You can review, edit or cancel it before sending.

The prepared report may contain the Deuce version and build number, device and operating-system information, StoreKit availability, Apple Watch delivery events, Shared Match connection diagnostics, and any message you add. It does not automatically attach your complete match history.

If you send a report, its contents and your email address will be received as support correspondence and may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to investigate the issue and maintain appropriate support records. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Sharing match results

When you choose to share a match result, Apple presents the system share sheet. Deuce shares only the content you deliberately select with the service or person you choose. Any receiving service is governed by its own privacy policy.

Apple services

Apple may process information when you download Deuce, make an in-app purchase, use Apple Watch communication, or use other Apple system services. That processing is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy. Deuce does not receive your full payment information.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Deuce does not include third-party advertising or analytics services and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual matches or clear Game History within Deuce. Depending on your app version, clearing Game History may also clear associated statistics and recent Shared Match players. You can remove other locally stored Deuce information by deleting the app from your devices.

To request deletion of information you deliberately sent in a support email, contact us using the address below.

Children’s privacy

Deuce is a family-friendly, general-purpose sports utility. It does not require an account and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through a developer-operated online service.

Contact

For privacy questions, support, or a deletion request, email SamSpencer@me.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Deuce or its privacy practices change. The latest version will be published on this page, together with its effective date.
