Frequently asked questions

How do I start a match on Apple Watch?

Open Deuce on your Watch, choose the match format, enter the player names and begin scoring. A completed Watch match will sync back to Game History on your iPhone.

My Watch match has not appeared on iPhone

Open Game History on iPhone and pull down to refresh, or tap the three-dot menu and choose Sync from Apple Watch. Keep Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi enabled on both devices.

How do I undo a point?

Tap the undo button during a live match. Deuce restores the previous scoring state, including game and set boundaries.

How do I share or delete a match?

In Game History, swipe a match row to reveal the Share and Delete actions.

Can I clear all saved matches?

Open Game History, tap the three-dot menu and choose Clear Game History…. Deuce will ask you to confirm before deleting anything.
